About Safe and Secure Solutions, Tumakuru

A local computer, laptop and printer service centre in Tumakuru (Tumkur), Karnataka.

What we do

Safe and Secure Solutions repairs and services laptops, desktops and printers, refills printer cartridges and helps customers with computer accessories and upgrades. We serve homes, students, offices and small businesses in Tumakuru.

Authorized service

We are an authorized service center for Dell, Lenovo, Acer and TVS-e. See our pages for Dell, Lenovo, Acer and TVS-e.

How we work

We diagnose the problem first, explain what we find and discuss the repair and cost before starting. We would rather give you a straight answer, including when a repair is not worthwhile, than sell you work you do not need.
